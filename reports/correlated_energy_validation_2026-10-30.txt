Correlated energy methods and compact UI validation — 2026-10-30

Scope
- Added CCSD(T), CASSCF and NEVPT2 to PySCF and Behemoth in the Quantum Chemistry panel.
- Added CASSCF and NEVPT2 to ORCA. ORCA and Psi4 already offered CCSD(T).
- Newly added methods are energy-only, as requested. Choosing one selects Energy;
  geometry and excited-state jobs are disabled for that selection.
- CASSCF/NEVPT2 require explicit active-electron and active-orbital settings.
  Validation checks spin parity, capacity, total electron count and paired inactive electrons.
- PySCF runs its coupled-cluster/multireference APIs; Behemoth receives a native input
  through the selected executable; ORCA receives a CASSCF block with SC_NEVPT2 when selected.
- NEVPT2 results use the corrected total energy. Failed/nonconverged PySCF or Behemoth
  correlated calculations do not silently fall back to a reference energy.
- Existing saved MethodConfig data loads with empty active-space settings.
- Compact Surface sliders retain editable values; Style and Lobe colours share one row.
- UV-Vis was removed from the QC quick buttons and remains in More….

calculation was part of this validation. Open-shell correlated cases were not run here.

An initial ORCA smoke test using STO-3G encountered the existing automatic STO-3G/C
auxiliary-basis spelling limitation. The test was changed to def2-SVP. No unrelated
basis-generator changes were made. The test's energy sanity range was then widened
for the larger basis. The final test run passed.
