Optimization-tolerance editor and diatomic selector
==================================================

Scope: Quantum Chemistry panel. Separate Clean Up Geometry, Conformer Search,
and TS Generation panels were not changed in this update.

Tolerances opens an independently resizable window. Draft values are applied
only by Save; Cancel or closing the window leaves settings intact. Reset
defaults resets the draft. Changing calculation/driver discards an incompatible
draft. Values are part of MethodConfig, so saved projects retain them and old
projects receive defaults through serde(default).

Controls and connections
------------------------
- Beavyr minimum/TS: energy, maximum/RMS displacement, maximum/RMS gradient.
- Beavyr MECP: energy gap and maximum/RMS displacement and effective gradient;
  Angstrom/Hartree-per-Angstrom conventions and inverse Hessian 0.7 preserved.
- ORCA native minimum/TS: documented TolE/TolMaxD/TolRMSD/TolMaxG/TolRMSG.
- ORCA native MECP: separate geometry thresholds and final energy-gap check.
- PySCF/geomeTRIC: convergence_* controls; displacement converted to Angstrom.
- Psi4: explicit *_g_convergence controls in atomic units; native defaults
  retained until custom criteria enabled. Default QCHEM does not use all five
  criteria; choosing custom values explicitly activates the supplied criteria.
- Native xTB: convergence-level presets; Beavyr driver permits individual
  numerical thresholds.
- DREIDING/Sparrow minimum jobs: values passed to the actual in-process solver.
- Relaxed scans: constraints retain their existing residual checks; adjustable
  optimization stopping criteria reach Beavyr, ORCA and Behemoth drivers.
- Behemoth CLI/input: five opt-* convergence flags and @opt underscore keys,
  applied to its own minimum, TS, and constrained-scan optimizers. No change
  to defaults when overrides are absent. Implementations remain in named files.

Diatomic PES sampling now uses mutually exclusive Harmonic / Morse buttons.
The existing Morse model and parameter calculations are unchanged.
