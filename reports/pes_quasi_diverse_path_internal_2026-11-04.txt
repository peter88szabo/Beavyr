PES sampling: quasi-random numbers, diverse subsets, reaction-path and
internal-coordinate sampling — 2026-11-04

Scope
-----
Four new options in the PES Sampling panel (ideas 7, 3, 1 and 2 of
~/Desktop/pes_sampling_feature_ideas_2026-11-04.txt).

| Option         | Choices                                        | Default            |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Random numbers | Quasi-random (scrambled Halton), Pseudo-random | Quasi-random       |
| Diverse subset | on/off, oversampling 1–100×                    | on, 3×             |
| Workflow       | + Reaction path                                | (Unimolecular)     |
| Coordinates    | Cartesian (rectilinear), Internal (curvilinear)| Cartesian          |

1. Quasi-random numbers (src/pes_sampling/quasi.rs)
---------------------------------------------------
- Scrambled Halton sequence: dimension k uses the k-th prime base with a
  seeded random digit permutation (π(0) = 0, Braaten–Weller style), which
  removes the strong correlations of plain Halton between large bases.
- Each sample attempt (accepted or rejected) is one sequence point; its random
  choices consume dimensions in a fixed order (mode energy draws and phases in
  mode order, TS displacement, rotor angles, orientations), so a given choice
  always lands on the same dimension. Up to 512 dimensions; further choices
  fall back to pseudo-random numbers.
- Repeatable: the scrambling is seeded by the run seed.
- Measured (4096 points, 8×8 bins, chi² ~ 63 ± 11 for independent draws):
  leading dimension pairs are far more even (test requires < 0.5× pseudo).
  Single high dimensions (k = 40, 120) remain stratified (< 0.25×).
  LIMITATION: pairs of high dimensions (e.g. 40/41, bases 179/181) were only
  mildly better (chi² 51 vs 77) — a known Halton property while N is below the
  product of the two bases. Sobol with Joe–Kuo direction numbers would do
  better there; not implemented (needs the direction-number table).

2. Diverse subset (src/pes_sampling/diverse.rs)
-----------------------------------------------
- Generates ceil(oversampling × N) candidates per block, keeps N by
  farthest-point selection on all inverse interatomic distances (Å⁻¹; f32
  storage), starting from the candidate nearest the mean descriptor.
- Blocks: the whole set (unimolecular), each COM distance (bimolecular), each
  path point (reaction path) — grids keep exact counts and order.
- Output is in selection order: any prefix is itself well spread.
- Guards: oversampling 1–100; ≤ 20 M candidate atom-frames; ≤ 50 M descriptor
  values per block; selection work Σ N·candidates·D ≤ 2e10.
- Progress bar counts candidates plus selection steps; cancellable.
- Note: farthest-point selection favours the edges of the sampled region and
  is not invariant to permuting identical atoms.

3. Reaction-path workflow (src/pes_sampling/path.rs)
----------------------------------------------------
- "Load path Hessians…" takes self-contained Hessians (ORCA .hess, Beavyr
  exports) along an IRC, ordered by natural file-name order (irc_2 < irc_10).
  All points must share atoms, order and masses; at least two points.
- Tangent at point k: neighbour geometries superposed onto point k
  (mass-weighted Kabsch via Horn's quaternion), central difference inside,
  one-sided at the ends; duplicate geometries are rejected.
- Projection: existing EckartMode::ReactionPath (translations, rotations and
  the tangent), giving 3N−7 modes per point; negative-curvature modes
  orthogonal to the path are excluded with a warning.
- One excitation setting and "samples per path point" for the whole path.
- XYZ comment: path_point=k path_points=K path_file=<name>
  path_projection=reaction_path_hamiltonian.

4. Internal-coordinate displacement (src/pes_sampling/curvilinear.rs)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- dx = Σ L_j Q_j (+ TS-mode displacement) → dq = B(x_eq) dx → geometry with
  q(x) = q_eq + dq, applied in substeps of ≤ 0.2 bohr/rad, each solved by
  Gauss–Newton with the B matrix at the current geometry,
  dx = (BᵀB)⁺ Bᵀ r in Cartesian space. Intermediate substeps converge to
  1e-5 bohr, the last to 1e-9 bohr. Rotors are applied afterwards.
- Internals from optimizer/internal_coords.rs (bonds, angles, dihedrals);
  bonds from covalent radii × 4/3; near-linear bends (< ~3°) and dihedrals
  through them are dropped. Refused with a clear message for linear molecules
  and when the internals span fewer than 3N−6 directions (disconnected parts).
- Supporting refactor: optimizer::internal_coords::wilson_b (B matrix only)
  split out of bpg_matrix; bpg_matrix behaviour unchanged.
- Development notes: the IRC connectivity model alone found no O–H bond in
  water (bond only below 0.95 × the radius sum), hence the 4/3 scaling. A
  fixed-B iteration did not converge on the 19-atom TS example at 500 K
  (corrections up to 0.8 bohr), hence substeps + updated B.
- Cost (debug build): 19-atom TS, 150 candidates 70 s with internal-space
  pseudo-inverse, 14 s after the Cartesian-space solve. Release builds are
  typically 20–50× faster. Not measured in release.

Other changes
-------------
- sampling.rs: sampling now runs per block with a Draws stream; energy() and
  morse_level() accept any uniform source (trait quasi::Unit).
- input.rs: from_hessian_on_path (reaction-path projection).
- output.rs: Sample.path_point, SamplingOutput.path_names, comment fields.
- XYZ comments now also record random=scrambled_halton|xoshiro256,
  coordinates=cartesian|internal_curvilinear and the diverse-subset setting.
- README: PES section describes the four options; options table extended.
