Source strategy
---------------
Pebble supplies the overall unimolecular and fixed-distance bimolecular
Monte Carlo workflows. Smite supplies the mature implementation conventions:
/home/peter/Dropbox/Research_Leuven/Smite/src/sampling/polyvibration.py
/home/peter/Dropbox/Research_Leuven/Smite/src/sampling/diatom.py
/home/peter/Dropbox/Research_Leuven/Smite/src/sampling/morse_diatom.py
/home/peter/Dropbox/Research_Leuven/Smite/src/sampling/thermal.py
/home/peter/Dropbox/Research_Leuven/Smite/src/utils/euler.py

Every real vibrational mode receives an independent phase uniform on [0,2*pi).
For harmonic modes: Q = sqrt(2E)/omega * cos(phi). Cartesian displacements
use the un-mass-weighted, mass-orthonormal mode matrix. This gives the QCT
coordinate distribution at the selected amplitude; it is not uniform in Q.
The unbounded Smite thermal option draws a new quantum number for every mode
and sample using floor[-kBT log(1-u)/omega], including zero-point energy.
Euler rotations use uniform phi/chi and uniform cos(theta) in [-1,1].

User controls
-------------
- Unimolecular sample count.
- Bimolecular first/last COM distance in Angstrom, number of distance points,
  and independent Monte Carlo samples per distance. Endpoints are included;
  one distance point means the first distance. Descending scans also work.
- ORCA Hessian with embedded equilibrium geometry and isotope masses, or a
  bare Turbomole-style $hessian in Eh/bohr^2 with matching equilibrium XYZ.
- Diatomic elements, equilibrium distance (Angstrom), frequency (cm^-1).
  Optional J=0 Morse oscillator adds dissociation energy De (eV); beta follows
  from the small-amplitude frequency and reduced mass. Atomic partners and
  rigid partner geometries are also supported.
- Global/common per-mode excitation limits, with optional individual mode
  overrides: ZPE, maximum quantum number, maximum energy in kcal/mol, or Tmax.
  Global energy means the same energy limit per mode, not a total-system cap.
- Three selectable temperature rules:
  1. Boltzmann-tail cutoff: minimal N with 1-exp[-(N+1)omega/(kBT)] >= p.
     Retained population p defaults to 99.9% and is editable. The resulting
     upper-state amplitude is sampled with a uniform phase.
  2. ZPE + kB*Tmax: continuous energy ceiling; quantum numbers need not be
     integral when the user specifies an energy rather than a quantum state.
  3. Smite thermal sampling without a hard harmonic cutoff. For Morse,
     canonical populations use only its physically bound states.
- Repeatable seed; atom-pair overlap filter enabled by default, using Pebble's
  cutoffs: H-H 0.4 Å, H-other 0.5 Å, other-other 0.8 Å. Checks all pairs,
  including pairs between collision partners; cutoffs remain editable.
  Filtering changes the accepted distribution and reports rejected attempts.

Geometry and output behavior
----------------------------
Each sampled partner is re-centered about its mass-weighted COM. Bimolecular
partners receive independent rotations and are translated along z to the exact
requested COM separation; the total COM stays at the origin. Rotation after
sampling rotates both equilibrium geometry and mode displacement together,
which is equivalent to rotating the normal-mode basis before sampling.

Translations/rotations are projected out before mode sampling. Imaginary modes
are excluded as in Smite and are listed in a copyable popup and results notice.
The source Hessian's raw harmonic frequencies are used. Bare matrices must
match the supplied equilibrium geometry and atom order. Dummy atoms in XYZ
inputs are discarded; Hessians themselves must describe real atoms only.

Generation runs in the background with progress and cancellation. Failed or
cancelled runs retain previous completed results. On success, all samples load
automatically into Beavyr and open the Trajectory panel, stopped at frame one
in Once mode. Save XYZ exports the complete sampled coordinates in double
precision; Load as Trajectory reloads the retained dataset. XYZ comments
include seed, phase distribution, sample number and, for bimolecular data,
COM distance, distance index, within-distance index and partner atom boundary.
Saving uses a temporary file and rename so a failed write does not truncate
an existing export. No quantum-chemistry calculations or MD propagation are
performed; the exported frames are sampled configurations, not dynamical time
steps. No PES energy labels are fabricated.

Compatibility and limits
------------------------
The existing 15-tab project layout representation is unchanged; the new tool
has its own open state. Old project layout deserialization is tested.
Project replacement is blocked during an active sampling run, consistent with
other running calculations. Sampling settings/results are not additional
project fields: save the sampled XYZ explicitly. Automatic preview is limited
to five million atom-frames. An impossible overlap filter stops after 1000
attempts per requested sample rather than hanging or silently omitting points.
Harmonic configurations with nonpositive diatomic bond lengths are rejected
with an actionable error; Morse states at/above dissociation are refused.

Validation
----------
Twelve PES-specific tests cover:
- Uniform phase statistics (100,000 samples), amplitude bound and harmonic
  Q/P energy conservation; <Q^2>/A^2 = 1/2 rather than the uniform-Q value 1/3.
- Exact Boltzmann cutoff coverage and minimal N; ZPE+kBT; unbounded thermal
  mean population and samples above the 99.9% threshold.
- Isotropic orientation moments (50,000 rotations) and length preservation.
- Bimolecular sample count/order, descending distances, exact COM spacing,
  independent partner orientations, global COM and repeatable seed.
- Diatomic mass normalization, isotope frequency shift and linear projection.
- Morse turning-point bounds and rejection of unbound states.
- Per-mode limits overriding global values.
- The real examples/TS_Gamma-3-6_ZZ-S_11_Compound_1.hess: 19 atoms, 50 sampled
  positive modes, one excluded imaginary mode and 20 generated configurations.
- Cancellation, invalid coordinates, zero sample count and impossible filters.
- Automatic trajectory-load behavior, stopped Once playback and XYZ round-trip.
- UI control rendering and backward-compatible project layout parsing.
