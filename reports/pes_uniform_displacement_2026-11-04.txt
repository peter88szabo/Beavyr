PES sampling: uniform mode displacement option — 2026-11-04

Scope
-----
Add an even ("uniform in amplitude") normal-mode displacement distribution to
PES Sampling, make it the default, and keep the previous cosine-weighted
(classical phase) sampling as a selectable alternative.

Problem
-------
Each mode was displaced as Q = A·cos(2πu), A = sqrt(2E)/ω, u ~ U[0,1).
A uniform phase is a uniform time along the classical trajectory, so Q follows
the arcsine density 1/(π·sqrt(A² − Q²)): samples crowd near the turning points
±A and the bottom of the well is sparsely sampled (the central fifth of
[−A, A] receives only 2/π·asin(0.2) ≈ 12.8 % of samples). That is correct for
QCT initial conditions, but PES sampling is not dynamics and needs the
low-energy region covered as well.

Change
------
New setting "Mode displacement" (toggle buttons in the PES Sampling panel,
above "Repeatable"), applied to every vibrating partner in both workflows:

- Uniform in amplitude (default): Q = A·(2u − 1). Even coverage of [−A, A]
  (20 % in the central fifth).
- Classical phase (cos): Q = A·cos(2πu), unchanged previous behaviour.

Morse diatomics:
- Uniform: r uniform between the exact inner and outer classical turning
  points, r_in + u·(r_out − r_in).
- Classical phase: unchanged. Note that the existing Smite formula
  ξ = e^{−β(r−re)} = (1 − ε)/(1 + sqrt(ε)·sin φ), ε = E/De, is the exact
  time-dependent classical Morse solution, so a uniform φ gives the exact
  time-weighted Morse distribution.

Unchanged: TS-direction sampling (already uniform within its bound), free
rotors (uniform 0–360°), energy selection rules, overlap filter, Euler
rotations and COM placement.

Semantics to note
-----------------
- In both modes no sample lies beyond the classical turning points of the
  selected energy (no quantum tunnelling tails; this is not Wigner sampling).
- With uniform displacement the selected energy is a per-mode ceiling, not the
  exact energy of that mode in each sample.
- With many modes the summed Cartesian displacement still concentrates toward
  equilibrium (sum of independent contributions).

Provenance
----------
XYZ comment lines record the choice:
  displacement=uniform_within_turning_points   (uniform)
  phases=uniform_0_2pi                         (classical phase, as before)

Testing
-------

- Settings default is Uniform.
- 20 000 uniform draws: every tenth of [−A, A] holds 1800–2200; |Q| ≤ A.
- Classical draws: central fifth holds 2300–2850 of 20 000 (≈ 12.8 %).
- Morse uniform endpoints satisfy V(r) = E to 1e-12 on both sides of re;
  u = 0.5 gives the midpoint.
Existing tests (diatomic mass normalization, per-mode overrides, rotors, TS
mode, bimolecular COM, arcsine/energy conservation of harmonic_state) pass

Open items from the PES code review (not changed here)
------------------------------------------------------
1. A coordinate-free Hessian (Smite numeric, $hessian, Behemoth) silently uses
   the partner's retained geometry, which can be stale (set by "Sample PES",
   "Use current geometry" or an earlier XYZ). Same atom count → wrong modes
   without a warning; the geometry label is hidden once a Hessian is loaded.
2. "Use current geometry" takes the current trajectory frame, which after a
   run is a displaced sample, not the equilibrium.
3. $frequency_scale_factor from ORCA .hess is applied by Frequency Analysis
   but not by PES sampling.
4. The "Sample TS direction" checkbox stays clickable under Rigid geometry.
5. Re-enabling "Set limits per mode" discards earlier per-mode edits.
6. Very soft unassigned modes (< ~50 cm⁻¹) produce large rectilinear
   displacements; a warning or rotor suggestion would help.
