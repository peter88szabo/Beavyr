Notes

The teeth are orange on purpose. Beaver incisors really are — the enamel is reinforced with iron, which is what lets them cut hardwood without the edge wearing flat. It is the one colour the animal actually gives you, so it is the accent throughout.

Each mark sits on a rounded pale tile. That tile is part of the icon: application menus and docks are dark on some desktops and light on others, and a bare dark outline disappears against the first kind. Whichever you choose, a dark-tile version is a two-minute change.

What makes a beaver read as a beaver is the silhouette, not the face: body twice as long as it is tall, legs barely showing, and a tail wide enough to be unmistakable. The ear is deliberately small and set back — big round ears on top of the head are what turned the first attempt into a bear.
